Project meaning and purpose

About SecurePay AI

SecurePay AI solves a practical financial cybersecurity problem: identifying fraudulent card payments early enough for an analyst or bank system to review, verify, hold, or approve the transaction responsibly.

Main Task

Input transaction features, scale them with the trained scaler, classify the payment with a Random Forest model, and return fraud probability, risk level, recommendation, and audit history.

Why It Matters

Fraud losses are expensive and real fraud cases are rare, so the app focuses on probability-based triage, review workflow, and evidence storage instead of only showing a prediction label.

Model

Random Forest classifier with 30 transaction signals: Time, V1-V28, and Amount.

Predictions return a fraud/genuine label, probability, risk level, and operational recommendation.

Render Runtime

The production process is Gunicorn serving Flask. Render builds dependencies from requirements.txt and starts app:app.

Health checks run through the /health endpoint.
